# S Clinics Doctor AI Image Direction

## Approved direction

Create three photorealistic AI image concepts using the supplied doctor collage as the identity reference. Preserve the doctor's recognizable facial structure, short dark hair, trimmed beard, warm smile, and black clinical-scrub styling while avoiding fabricated medical claims or patient-identifying content.

## Image set

1. **Cinematic hero portrait** — the doctor standing confidently in a refined S Clinics interior, warm gold-and-charcoal lighting, vertical composition with clean negative space for the Arabic hero copy.
2. **Clinical consultation portrait** — the doctor inside a modern dental clinic, engaged and approachable, holding a clipboard or standing near a consultation area, no patient visible.
3. **Lifestyle brand portrait** — a lively candid moment of the doctor moving through the clinic or leaning casually in the branded space, natural smile and energetic but premium mood.

## Shared visual rules

- Editorial, premium, warm, and human; compatible with the existing black, champagne, and pearl palette.
- Natural anatomy, realistic hands, realistic dental-clinic details, and consistent facial identity across all three images.
- No text, logos, watermarks, readable signage, extra people, patients, procedures, blood, or exaggerated dental props.
- Generate as website-ready raster assets; inspect each output before selecting it for the project.

## Intended placement

The strongest vertical portrait replaces the current hero doctor image. The clinical portrait supports the doctor section or a secondary hero slide. The lifestyle portrait supports the carousel or a future social/intro section.
